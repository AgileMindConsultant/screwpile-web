What to include

If something is broken, these four things let us find it much faster:

  • Your device model (for example "Pixel 7" or "iPhone 13")
  • Your Android or iOS version
  • The Screwpile version, shown in the app's settings screen
  • What you were doing when it happened, and the level number if it was in a level

Purchases and refunds

Purchases made inside Screwpile are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store, not by AgileMind. The store holds the payment and the billing record, so refund requests have to go through Google Play support or Apple support directly — we cannot issue a refund on their behalf. If a purchase was charged but the item never unlocked, email us and we will help you sort it out.

Restoring a purchase

If you reinstall Screwpile or move to a new device, sign in to the same Google Play or Apple account you bought with and your purchases — including No Ads — will be restored by the store.

Lost progress

Screwpile saves your progress on your device only; there is no account and no cloud save at launch. That means uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, removes your progress permanently, and progress does not follow you to a new device. We are sorry — we cannot restore it from our side, because we never receive it.

Ads

You can turn off personalized ads at any time in your device settings — on Android under Settings → Google → Ads, and on iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You will still see ads, but they will not be based on your advertising ID. If you would rather not see ads at all, the No Ads purchase removes them.
