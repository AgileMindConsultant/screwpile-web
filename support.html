This is a placeholder page. Screwpile has not been released yet. A working support contact will be published here before the app is submitted to any app store.

What will be published here

This URL is the one that will be filed as the Support URL in Apple App Store Connect and as the support contact on the Google Play listing. The finished page will carry a monitored contact address, the information we need in order to help with a problem (device, OS version, app version), and answers to the questions players actually ask most.

Purchases and refunds

Purchases made inside the app are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store, not by AgileMind. Refunds and billing records are held by the store, so those requests have to go through Google Play support or Apple support directly.
