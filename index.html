Screwpile — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-17

This policy explains what data Screwpile collects and how it is used.

Who controls this data

Screwpile is made by AgileMind. For privacy questions, contact info@agilemindconsulting.com.

What we collect

Advertising. Screwpile shows ads (rewarded, interstitial and banner) through Google AdMob. AdMob collects your advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and app-interaction data, and uses them for ad delivery, frequency capping and fraud prevention. Screwpile never requests GPS or precise location. In the EEA and the UK a Google-certified consent prompt asks before you are shown personalized ads; on iOS the App Tracking Transparency prompt asks before your advertising ID is used for targeting. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized and your advertising ID is not used for targeting.

Purchases. If you buy No Ads, a Starter Pack, coin packs or a season pass, the payment is processed entirely by Google Play Billing on Android or Apple's StoreKit on iOS. We receive only the result of the purchase — what you bought — so the app can unlock it. We never see your payment method, card number or billing address. The stores' own policies cover their part of the transaction:

  • Google Play: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple App Store: apple.com/legal/privacy

Gameplay analytics. We use Firebase Analytics to understand how people play: which levels are started, completed and failed, which boosters are used, ad and purchase funnel events, and session length. This is tied to a pseudonymous per-install identifier, not to you personally. We do not link this analytics data to advertising identifiers for ad targeting.

Crash reporting. We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash logs along with basic device and app information, so that we can find and fix bugs. This is used for diagnostics only and is never used for advertising.

No accounts, no personal details. Screwpile has no sign-in, no profile, and no email or username field. It does not collect your name, contacts, photos, calendar, phone number or precise location.

How long it is kept, and who it goes to

All of the data above is collected by Google's SDKs and sent to Google, which processes it on our behalf and under its own retention schedules. We hold no separate copy of it and operate no server of our own. All SDK traffic is encrypted in transit over HTTPS.

Your choices

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalization entirely, in your device settings — Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Because Screwpile has no account, there is no profile for us to export or delete. Purchase records belong to your Google Play or Apple account and are managed by the store, not by us.

Children

Screwpile is a general-audience game and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if a future release changes what Screwpile collects — for example adding accounts, cloud save, or a new third-party SDK — and we will change the "Last updated" date above when we do.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@agilemindconsulting.com. See also the support page.
