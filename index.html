Screwpile — Privacy Policy

Status: placeholder · Last updated: 2026-11-17

This is a placeholder page. Screwpile has not been released yet. The final privacy policy is being prepared and will be published here before the app is submitted to any app store. Nothing on this page should be read as a description of what a shipped Screwpile build does or does not collect.

Who this is

Screwpile is a mobile puzzle game made by AgileMind. This page is the permanent home of its privacy policy, and this URL is the one that will be filed in the Google Play Data safety form and in Apple App Store Connect.

What will be published here

The finished policy will state, for the actual shipping build: what data the app and its third-party SDKs collect, what each item is used for, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how to contact us about it. It will be written against a code-level review of the real build rather than from a template, and it will be updated whenever a release changes what the app collects.

Contact

Until the policy is published, questions can go to the contact details on the Screwpile store listing once the app is live. See also the support page.
